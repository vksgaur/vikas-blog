About

This is Vikas Gaur’s corner of the internet.

I like to read deeply, think slowly, and understand the world properly rather than quickly. In a world that rushes everything, I find joy in sitting with an idea long enough for it to actually make sense.

I write about history, economics, technology, and the patterns that connect them. My long-form research reports live at vikas-reports. This blog is where the shorter, more personal writing goes — essays, observations, and the occasional argument with myself.

I believe that the best ideas are worth more than five minutes of attention, and that understanding anything properly requires the patience to read slowly, think carefully, and change your mind when the evidence demands it.
